# Past Year Reflection — Guided Self-Interview

This is a conversation with yourself. Read each question, sit with it for a moment, and write your honest answer. There are no right answers. The goal is to surface what you already know but haven't said out loud.

You can do this in one sitting (60-90 minutes) or spread it across several days, a few questions at a time.

Write as much or as little as feels right. Some questions will hit hard. Some won't land at all. That's fine.

---

## Setting the Stage

Before you start, take two minutes. Close your laptop for a moment. Take a few breaths. This isn't a performance review. No one is grading this. This is you, talking to you, about a year that actually happened.

Ready? Let's begin.

---

## Part 1: The Big Picture

**Tell me about the last year — highlights first. What went well? What are you genuinely glad happened?**



---

**Now the other side. What was hard? What hurt? What didn't go the way you wanted?**



---

**If you had to describe this year in a single sentence — not a positive spin, just the truth — what would you say?**



---

**What would your spouse say about this year? Not what you hope they'd say — what would they actually say?**



---

## Part 2: Energy and Time

**Where did your energy go this year? Not your time — your energy. What consumed you mentally and emotionally?**



---

**What drained you the most? The thing that took more out of you than it gave back?**



---

**What gave you energy? What activities, people, or moments recharged you?**



---

**Where did time leak? Those hours or days that just disappeared into things that didn't matter?**



---

**Did you rest enough? Not "did you take vacation" — did you actually rest? Did you recover?**



---

## Part 3: Decisions and Avoidance

**What was the best decision you made this year?**



---

**What was the worst decision, or the decision you most regret?**



---

**Where did you avoid hard decisions? What conversations did you not have, what actions did you not take, because they were uncomfortable?**



---

**Is there something you've been putting off that you know you need to face? What is it?**



---

**If you had made that hard decision six months ago, where would you be now?**



---

## Part 4: Relationships

**How is your marriage, honestly? Not the version you present to friends — the real version, between you two.**



---

**What does your spouse need from you that you're not giving?**



---

**How are you as a dad right now? Where are you showing up well? Where are you falling short?**



---

**Is there a relationship you let slip this year that you wish you hadn't?**



---

**Who showed up for you this year? Who surprised you with their support?**



---

## Part 5: Identity

**Who were you this year? Not your title or role — who were you as a person?**



---

**What are you proud of that no one else sees? The private win that didn't make it into any conversation.**



---

**What would you not repeat under any circumstances? The experience, pattern, or choice you refuse to relive.**



---

**Did you grow this year? In what way?**



---

**What did you learn about yourself that you didn't know before?**



---

## Part 6: The Honest Test

**If this year repeated ten times, would you be satisfied? Would that be a good life?**



---

**If someone you deeply respect could see exactly how you spent this year — every day, every choice, every moment — would you feel proud?**



---

**What's the thing you most need to hear right now? The thing you'd tell a close friend in your exact situation?**



---

## Closing

Take a moment. Read back through your answers. Highlight or star anything that surprised you — anything that came out that you didn't expect to say.

**What's the single biggest takeaway from this reflection?**



---

**What do you want to do about it?**
