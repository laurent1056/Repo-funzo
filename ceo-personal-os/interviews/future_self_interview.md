# Future Self Interview — Guided Self-Interview

This interview is different from the others. Here, you're not reflecting on the past or examining the present. You're having a conversation with the person you want to become.

The concept is simple: imagine yourself five years from now, sitting across from your current self. That future version has made it through the transition. They found the right job. They deepened the marriage. They showed up for their kids. They took care of their body. They're calm, clear, and settled.

Now that person is going to answer your questions.

Write in the voice of your future self. Present tense. First person. As if you're already there.

---

## Setting the Scene

Close your eyes for thirty seconds. Picture yourself at 56. You've navigated the career transition. You're in a role you chose deliberately. Your marriage is in a different place — a better one. Your kids are older, and they respect you. Your health is solid. You're not anxious.

That person is sitting across from you right now. You can ask them anything.
