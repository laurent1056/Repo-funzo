# Identity and Values — Guided Self-Interview

This interview is about who you are underneath the roles. Not the CEO, not the founder, not the job title — the person. The goal is to articulate what you actually value, not what you think you should value.

Most people have never explicitly defined their values. They inherit them from parents, absorb them from culture, or perform them for social approval. This interview strips that away and asks: what's actually true for you?

Take your time. This isn't urgent. But it is important.
