## How to Use This Template

This annual review pulls together several frameworks from your system. You'll need these files open:

- `frameworks/annual_review.md` — The detailed Gustin-style reflection (complete that first)

- `frameworks/ideal_life_costing.md` — Lifestyle cost analysis
- `goals/1_year.md` — Last year's goals
- `memory.md` — Accumulated insights
- Your quarterly reviews from the past year

**Recommended order:**
1. Complete the `frameworks/annual_review.md` first (the deep reflection)
