# Annual Review Framework

*Adapted from Dr. Anthony Gustin's Annual Review process. Gustin's approach emphasizes structured, honest reflection across multiple life dimensions before setting any goals. The core insight: you can't plan a meaningful future without first understanding what actually happened.*

---

## What This Is

Most people set New Year's goals based on feelings — what they're excited about on January 1st. That excitement fades by February. This framework replaces excitement with understanding.

You will look back at the past year in detail, identify what worked, what didn't, and why, and only then decide what to do next. The review comes before the plan. Always.

## When to Use This

- During your annual review (last week of December or first week of January)
- Any time you feel lost and need to re-anchor
- After a major life transition (job change, move, health event)

## Time Required

Set aside 2-4 hours. Do it in one sitting if possible, or split across two sessions on consecutive days.
