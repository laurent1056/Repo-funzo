# Life Map

*Adapted from Alex Lieberman's Life Map framework. Lieberman argues that most people obsess over their career while neglecting the other dimensions that actually determine life satisfaction. The Life Map forces you to evaluate all six domains of your life at once — not to optimize them all simultaneously (that's impossible), but to see the full picture and make conscious trade-offs instead of unconscious ones.*

---

## What This Is

A Life Map is a snapshot of where you stand across six key domains. It is not a report card. It is a diagnostic tool — like an MRI for your life. The goal is honest awareness, not a perfect score.

The six domains:
1. **Career** — Your work, your professional identity, your income
2. **Relationships** — Your marriage, your friendships, your family bonds
3. **Health** — Your body, your energy, your physical capacity
4. **Meaning** — Your sense of purpose, contribution, and alignment
5. **Finances** — Your financial security, freedom, and trajectory
6. **Fun** — Your enjoyment, play, adventure, and aliveness

No one scores a 10 in all six at once. The map shows you where you're strong, where you're neglecting something, and where a small investment of attention could produce outsized returns.

## When to Use This

- During your annual review (mandatory)
- During quarterly reviews (recommended)
- Any time you feel "off" but can't articulate why

## How to Use This Going Forward

1. Complete a full Life Map during your annual review.
2. Do a quick score-only check during quarterly reviews.
3. Reference your Life Map when making big decisions: "How will this affect my weakest domain? My strongest?"
4. Store key insights in `memory.md` so patterns emerge over time.

The Life Map doesn't tell you what to do. It tells you where to look.
