# Three-Year Goals

**Set:** February 2026
**Time Horizon:** February 2029
**Review:** Annually, during your annual review

Three-year goals are about direction, not precision. You can't predict where you'll be in three years — but you can decide what you're aiming at. These goals should feel ambitious but not fantasy. They should connect to your Vivid Vision.

---

## The Three-Year Picture

By February 2029, I want to be able to say:

### Career
**I am in a role I chose, not one I settled for.** I've been in this position long enough to have built real credibility and impact. The work is challenging but not consuming. I'm compensated well — above my Ideal Life number. I've rebuilt my professional identity around what I do now, not what I used to do. The career transition of 2026 is a story I tell at dinner parties, not a wound I'm still nursing.

**Specific markers:**
- In a stable, fulfilling role for 2+ years
- Earning at or above my Ideal Life number
- Clear boundaries between work and home
- Known and respected in my professional community
- Not dreading Monday mornings

---

### Marriage and Family
**My marriage is the strongest it's been in years.** We went through a hard stretch — career uncertainty, financial stress, identity shifts — and we came out the other side closer. My wife would say I'm a different person in the ways that matter: more present, more honest, better at listening. My kids see a partnership they'd want to model.

**Specific markers:**
- Regular date nights or protected couple time (weekly minimum)
- We've had the hard conversations — not perfectly, but we've had them
- My spouse would independently describe the marriage as improving
- My kids see us as a team

---

### Parenting
**My kids know they can count on me.** They're older now — and they still come to me. Not because I'm perfect, but because I showed up consistently during a time when I could have retreated. I've modeled resilience, honesty, and self-care. They've seen me fail and get back up.

**Specific markers:**
- Regular one-on-one time with each child
- They come to me voluntarily — not just when they need something
- I can name their friends, their struggles, their interests
- I'm at their events, and they know I want to be there

---

### Health
**I'm in the best physical condition of my 50s.** Not bodybuilder condition — functional, energetic, sustainable condition. I exercise regularly without forcing it. I sleep well. My annual checkups are clean. I have the energy to keep up with my life and my family.

**Specific markers:**
- Consistent exercise habit (4-5x/week, two years running)
- Healthy weight, good bloodwork
- Sleeping 7+ hours consistently
- Energy is stable throughout the day
- No major deferred health issues

---

### Finances
**Financial stress is no longer a background hum.** The career transition didn't destroy us because we knew our number and planned accordingly. We're saving again. We have a real emergency fund. College planning is on track. I make financial decisions from clarity, not fear.

**Specific markers:**
- 6+ months of emergency fund
- Retirement savings back on track
- Debt reduced or eliminated
- Living within means, with margin for quality of life
- Know my numbers and review them quarterly

---

### Meaning and Fun
**I'm not just surviving — I'm engaged with life.** I have hobbies that aren't work. I have friendships that are real. I do things purely for fun, without guilt. I feel useful in ways that go beyond my job title. The restlessness of the transition has been replaced by a quieter kind of purpose.

**Specific markers:**
- At least one regular hobby or activity done purely for enjoyment
- Friendships that involve more than texting
- Occasional travel or adventure as a family
- A sense of contribution beyond work (mentoring, community, volunteering)

---

## Three-Year Review Questions

When you revisit this document in 2029:

- Which of these visions came closest to reality?
- Which one was I most wrong about?
- What happened that I couldn't have predicted?
- What would I tell the 2026 version of me?

---

*This document is a compass, not a contract. Review it during your annual review. Update it when your direction changes. The goal is to be pointed somewhere meaningful, not to predict the future perfectly.*
