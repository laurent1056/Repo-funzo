# North Star

Your North Star is a single statement that anchors every decision. It is not a goal — goals change. It is not a mission statement — those are for companies. It is the simplest possible answer to: "What am I optimizing for?"

---

## My North Star

**I am building a life where my family is proud to have me around, my body can keep up with my ambitions, and my work is something I chose — not something I settled for.**

---

## How to Use This

When you're confused about what to do next, read this sentence. Then ask:

- Does this decision move me toward or away from this?
- Would the version of me described here make this choice?
- If I do this, will I feel more aligned or less?

If your North Star no longer feels true, rewrite it. It should feel like a compass, not a cage.

---

## Checking Alignment

Every quarter, during your quarterly review, revisit this statement and ask:

1. Is this still true for me?
2. Did my behavior this quarter reflect this?
3. What's the biggest gap between this statement and how I actually lived?
